# Verification queue v1: bistro

Four contested terms, ordered by how many other entries depend on them. Each answer becomes one
file in `../../decisions/`, in the expert's own words.

## 1. Guest or customer on screens

- **Disciplines affected:** floor, engineering
- **Contested:** the floor never says customer. The app's booking screens and its database do.
  The glossary will name the entry Guest by the authority order either way.
- **Decision needed:** may "customer" appear on any screen a guest or a member of staff sees?
- **Depends on it:** Party, Cover, Allergy note

## 2. Server: the person or the computer

- **Disciplines affected:** floor, engineering
- **Contested:** "server timeout" froze the floor on a Friday, and staff read it as one of their
  servers. The issues and the code use the word for the computer.
- **Decision needed:** does the glossary define the computer at all, and what word does the app
  use for it in front of staff?
- **Depends on it:** Check, Tip

## 3. What a cover counts

- **Disciplines affected:** floor, engineering
- **Contested:** the handbook counts covers as guests who sat down. BIS-14 says covers is the
  party size on the reservation. v1 lists covers under Guest's `also_called`, which is probably
  wrong under both readings.
- **Decision needed:** is a cover a guest, a count of guests, or the party size? Does it need
  its own entry?
- **Depends on it:** nothing yet; Table and Turn would use it

## 4. Walk-in: one word, two things

- **Disciplines affected:** floor, kitchen
- **Contested:** the floor's walk-in is a party with no reservation. The kitchen's walk-in is the
  cold room. v1 holds it as a gap.
- **Decision needed:** two entries, or a new name for one of them?
- **Depends on it:** nothing yet
