# 002: A cover is a count of guests who sat down

## Question

Queue item 3, v1: the handbook counts covers as guests who sat down; BIS-14 says covers is the
party size on the reservation. Is a cover a guest, a count, or the party size?

## Ruling

"A cover is a head we count, after they sit down. Party size is what they booked. If six book
and five come, the booking said six and we did five covers. We don't serve covers, we serve
guests. Never use covers for the booking."

## Applies to

- [[Cover]]: new entry, `type: metric`, in Floor. It counts guests and measures a service.
- [[Guest]]: covers leaves `also_called`; `not_to_be_confused_with` gains [[Cover]].
