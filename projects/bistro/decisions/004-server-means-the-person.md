# 004: Server means the person

## Question

Queue item 2, v1: "server timeout" froze the floor and staff read it as one of their servers.
Does the glossary define the computer at all, and what does the app call it in front of staff?

## Ruling

"Fine. In anything the floor reads, a server is a person. The app's messages say 'the system'
now. The code keeps its names; nobody on the floor reads those, and the glossary doesn't need an
entry for our computer."

## Applies to

- [[Server]]: `status` becomes `verified`; the definition is the person only. The computer sense
  gets no entry.
