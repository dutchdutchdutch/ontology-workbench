# Core terms: Linden Street Bistro

The twelve words to learn first. Almost every other term in the glossary is built from these.

## People

| | |
|---|---|
| **Guest** | A person who comes to the restaurant to eat, from the moment they arrive until they leave. |
| **Party** | One or more [[Guest]]s who arrive together and eat together at one table. |
| **Server** | The person who works a [[Service]] looking after a section of [[Table]]s, takes each party's [[Order]] and brings its food. |

## The meal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reservation** | A promise, made for a named [[Party]], that holds a [[Table]] at a set time and falls in one [[Service]]. |
| **Seating** | The moment a [[Party]] is given a [[Table]] and sits down: the table its [[Reservation]] holds, or the next free one. |
| **Order** | Everything one [[Party]] asks for after its [[Seating]], grouped into [[Course]]s. |
| **Course** | Every [[Menu item]] that one table eats at the same time, such as starters, mains or dessert. |
| **Ticket** | The kitchen's printed copy of an [[Order]], with its items listed by [[Course]]. Each station that cooks part of the order gets its own copy. |
| **Check** | The bill for one [[Party]] that the [[Server]] presents at the end of the meal: every [[Menu item]] ordered, at its price, plus tax. A party may split it by guest. |

## Floor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Service** | One meal period, such as lunch or dinner, from the first seating to the last order. |
| **Table** | A numbered place in the dining room that seats one [[Party]] for a meal. It is named by its seats: a four-top seats four. |

## Kitchen

| | |
|---|---|
| **Menu item** | One dish or drink on the menu, with its price. |
