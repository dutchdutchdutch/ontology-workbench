# Linden Street Bistro: staff handbook

Owner: Theo (floor manager). Last edited 2026-08-20.

## Welcome

Welcome to the Bistro. We seat about ninety covers on a Friday and we want every one of them to
leave happy. Read this before your first service.

## Words we use

We never say customer on the floor. Everyone who walks in is a guest.

A party is the group that comes in together. They sit together and they pay together. A party of
one is still a party.

When we say "a six-top" we mean a table for six. When we count covers we count guests: a
six-top with five people at it is five covers.

A service is one meal period: lunch is one service, dinner is another. Each service starts with
the first seating and ends with the last order.

## Front of house

The host stands at the door, greets every party and keeps the reservation book. The host does
every seating, so nobody walks a party to a table on their own.

A reservation holds a table for a named party at a set time. If the party has not arrived 15
minutes after the time, the host marks a no-show and gives the table away.

A walk-in is a party that turns up without a reservation. Seat walk-ins at the bar first if the
floor is full.

Each server has a section of tables for the service. Your server takes the order, brings the food
and presents the check at the end. Servers keep their own tips.

We turn the deuces two or three times on a busy night. A turn is one party seated, fed and gone.

## Paying

The check lists everything the party ordered with its price, plus tax. Only Theo can comp an
item: a comp takes the item off the check, and we note why.

Split checks are fine. Ask before you split, not after.

## Kitchen, for the floor

Never walk into the kitchen to ask for a dish. Talk to the expo at the pass.

If the kitchen 86es something, it is gone for the rest of the service. Tell your tables before
they order it.

Allergies go on the order as an allergy note, every time. The kitchen sees it in red on the
ticket.
