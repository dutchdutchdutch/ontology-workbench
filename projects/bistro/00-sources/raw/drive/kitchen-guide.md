# Kitchen guide

Owner: Rosa (head chef). Last edited 2026-08-28.

## The line

The line has four stations: grill, saute, cold and pastry. Each station cooks its own menu items
and has its own ticket printer. When a plate is finished, the station sends it to the pass.

The pass is the heated counter between the kitchen and the dining room. Plates wait there for the
expo, never longer than a minute.

## Tickets

Every order prints as a ticket at each station that cooks part of it. The ticket lists the items
by course, starters first. Some of you call them chits. Same thing.

The expo reads every ticket, calls fire for each course and checks every plate before a server
takes it.

## Courses and firing

A course is everything one table eats at the same time: starters, mains, dessert. We fire a
course when the expo calls it, not before. Fire mains when the starters are cleared, so they land
hot.

## Running out

When we run out of a menu item, the expo calls "86 the duck" and it is gone until the next
service. Somebody tells the floor right away.

## Allergies

An allergy note prints in red on every ticket for that order. Read it out loud at the pass before
the plate goes.

## Walk-in

The walk-in is our cold room. Close the walk-in door every time. Every station restocks from the
walk-in before service.

## Family meal

Family meal is at 4:15 before dinner service. Whoever is on cold cooks it. Everyone eats.
